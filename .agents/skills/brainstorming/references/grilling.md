## Grill method

- Questioning belongs to the independent [grill Skill](../../grill/SKILL.md).
- Brainstorming owns continuing draft context and design. New work and an existing Change's Replan may explore through a draft before their explicit handoff Gate.
- Use [explaining-work](../../explaining-work/SKILL.md) to make the relevant system concrete before asking and to show the resulting architecture and terms after each answer.
- Preserve key decisions and provenance; do not require full conversation duplication.
