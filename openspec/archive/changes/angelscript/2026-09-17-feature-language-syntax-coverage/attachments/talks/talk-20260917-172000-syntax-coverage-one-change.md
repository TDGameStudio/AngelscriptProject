# Full-scale reconstruction stays one Change

## Context

After measuring admitted Language, two-thirds of positive files have ≤5 `@begin` cases. A short "split six files" plan understated the work. The user asked not to underestimate, then chose one long Change that later subagents may run in parallel.

## Evidence

53 positive files / 324 cases; 35 positives ≤5 cases. Second-wave flats hold 87 positives. `Arithmetic` mashups every operator. `FunctionReturn` repeats `return 42`. Draft log R6–R9. [real-scale.md](../drafts/findings/real-scale.md), [parallel-dag.md](../drafts/findings/parallel-dag.md).

## Options

One small Change; one full-scale Change; several Changes (one chapter each, or three waves).

## Settled Decision

One Change `angelscript/feature-language-syntax-coverage` does the full reconstruction: six second-wave chapters, all 35 thin first-wave positives, and the Interface chapter. Removed syntax stays out. Author chapters are parallel. Generate, spec, Skill, and corpus join at the end.

## Consequences and Flip Condition

Ensure plan must list every `@begin` on each card. Flip: shared-file collisions under parallel edits, or a DAG that cannot be planned, force a replan that splits Changes.

## Visual

Author chapters fan out; generate and corpus rejoin. See [parallel-dag.md](../drafts/findings/parallel-dag.md).
