# Canonical native value-object receiver gate（2026-08-25）

## Scope

This AST-first gate advances Tasks `0.2`, `5.3`, `5.7`, `5.9`, `9.5`, `13.2`, and `13.6` for the next uniform failure exposed by the real StaticJIT generation source graph: `_Inner.IsBound()` inside the preprocessor-generated delegate `Execute` method.

The preceding cards prove direct construction/destruction of a 32-byte native non-POD member and exact native `opAssign`. This card proves that an ordinary native method call on that already-live member consumes the sealed receiver as an address. It is not a copy-construction, by-value argument, or return-value card.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- Do not classify `_FScriptDelegate` or the test fixture as POD/trivially-copyable.
- Do not add a 32-byte read, copy, widened `RDR`, or `memcpy` path.
- Do not synthesize a temporary copy merely to obtain a receiver pointer.
- Do not have CodeGen locate `IsBound`/`ReadStored` by spelling or rerun overload resolution.
- Do not use `asCCompiler`, HIR, or a LEGACY fallback.
